# Elden Ring artwork

The 4 wallpapers are art inspired by Elden Ring, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to FromSoftware and Bandai Namco Entertainment. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-the-elden-ring.jpg`: The Elden Ring above the Lands Between; artist unknown
- `02-shadow-of-the-erdtree.jpg`: Shadow of the Erdtree; artist unknown
- `03-night-sky.jpg`: The stars over the Lands Between; artist unknown
- `04-malenia.jpg`: Malenia, Blade of Miquella; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: dark moss background, parchment text, grace gold accent, scarlet rot red, Limgrave green, Erdtree gold, glintstone blue, night violet and spirit teal.
